FAQ
How long does delivery take?

To the United States, 10–20 business days from the day you order, processing included. To Spain, 8–15 business days. You'll get a tracking number by email.

🎄 For Christmas, US orders should be placed by November 25.

Is shipping free?

Yes, on US orders over $30 (and Spanish orders over €25). On smaller orders, the cost is shown before you pay.

Do I save if I buy several items?

Yes, no code needed: 10% off when you buy 2 items and 15% off when you buy 3 or more. Mix and match any products and you'll see the discount in your cart.

Can I use a discount code?

Yes. Codes don't stack with the multi-buy discounts: the system automatically applies whichever saves you more. Free shipping still applies either way.

How are they powered?

Most run on batteries, so you can place them anywhere with no cords. Some, like the Magic Snowfall Projector and the Magic 2-in-1 Projector, plug into USB: a phone charger or a power bank. Each product page tells you how it's powered.

Can I use them outdoors?

Only the ones marked as waterproof or suitable for outdoor use. Projectors are best indoors or in covered areas. Everything else is for indoor or sheltered spaces.

Can I return an item?

Yes, you have 14 days from delivery, no questions asked. If something arrives damaged or doesn't work, send us a photo and we'll replace it or refund you at no cost. More details in Shipping & Returns.

Is it safe to pay here?

Yes. Payments are processed through Shopify's secure checkout and we never see your card details.

How do I contact you?

Use our contact page or email bodaboom1@gmail.com. We reply within 24 hours on business days.
