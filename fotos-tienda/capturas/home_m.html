    Contact us
    📦How long does delivery take?
    Delivery takes 10–20 business days from the day you order, processing included. When it ships, we email you the tracking number. For Christmas, order by November 25.
    🚚How much is shipping?
    It's free on all orders over $30, applied automatically at checkout.
    🛒Do I save if I buy several?
    Yes, no code needed: 10% off when you buy 2 items and 15% off when you buy 3 or more. You'll see it in your cart.
    🔋How are they powered?
    Most run on batteries, so you can place them anywhere with no cords. Some, like the Magic Snowfall Projector, plug into USB: a phone charger or a power bank. Each product page tells you how it's powered.
    🌧️Can I use them outdoors?
    Only the ones marked as waterproof or suitable for outdoor use, like the Giant LED Spider Web or the Fairy Micro Lights. Projectors are best indoors or in covered areas. Everything else is for indoor or sheltered spaces.
    ↩️Can I return my order?
    Yes, you have 14 days from delivery. If something arrives damaged, send us a photo and we'll replace it or refund you. More details in Shipping & Returns.
    🔒Is it safe to pay here?
    Yes. Payments go through Shopify's secure checkout and we never see your card details.
